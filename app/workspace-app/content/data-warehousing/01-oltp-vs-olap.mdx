The wide rows, rebuilt from the four tables:

```sql
SELECT o.order_id, i.line_no, o.created_at::date AS ordered_on,
       c.customer_id, c.country, p.product_id, p.category_id, i.quantity, i.unit_cents
FROM public.orders o
JOIN public.customers   c ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id
JOIN public.order_items i ON i.order_id    = o.order_id
JOIN public.products    p ON p.product_id  = i.product_id
WHERE o.order_id = 4473
ORDER BY i.line_no;
```
