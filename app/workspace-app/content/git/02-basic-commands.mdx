## The commands we use

<table>
  <thead><tr><th>You type</th><th>What it does</th><th>When</th></tr></thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr><td><code>git clone &lt;address&gt;</code></td><td>copies a repository, with its history, into a new folder</td><td>once, to get the course</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>git pull</code></td><td>brings in the commits added since your copy was made</td><td>when the instructor says there is an update</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>git status</code></td><td>says where every changed file stands, and what to type next</td><td>between any two other commands</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>git diff</code></td><td>shows the lines that changed and are not staged yet</td><td>before <code>add</code></td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>git add &lt;file&gt;</code></td><td>puts a change into the draft of the next commit</td><td>when you want to keep it</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>git commit -m "…"</code></td><td>records the draft as a snapshot, with a message saying why</td><td>one finished idea at a time</td></tr>
    <tr><td><code>git log --oneline</code></td><td>lists the snapshots, newest first</td><td>to see what was recorded</td></tr>
  </tbody>
</table>
